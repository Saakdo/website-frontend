Keing Rungruang Company

Automotive and Air Compressor Parts Manufacturing Service

Keing Rungruang Autoparts Company was established in 2001 by Mr. Yuttana Thiraprarom, the company's founder and owner. Initially operating as a small enterprise near the heart of Nakhon Ratchasima province, we began with just 30 dedicated employees. Our commitment to honest work and an unwavering emphasis on quality have been the cornerstone of every product we manufacture.


Over the years, we have grown significantly, leading to the relocation of our operations to the industrial zone in T. Tha-Ang. Today, we specialize in manufacturing services, offering a diverse range of products including automotive parts and air compressors.


At Keing Rungruang Autoparts, we pride ourselves on maintaining the highest standards of quality and reliability in our manufacturing processes. Our dedication to excellence is reflected in the certifications we have achieved, including:


  • ISO 9001:2015 - Demonstrating our commitment to quality management systems and continuous improvement.
  • IATF 16949:2016 - Acknowledging our adherence to the highest global standards in automotive manufacturing.
